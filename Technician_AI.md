# Technician AI Copilot Engine Specification

This document details the Gemma-powered copilots running inside the **Technician Workspace**.

## 1. Copilots Design

### Torque Guidelines & Manual Lookup
- Matches vehicle models to exact mechanical specifications (e.g. EV battery mounting bolts: 45 Nm) dynamically.

### Troubleshooting Recommendations
- Analyzes complaints to point out historic fault points (e.g. EV isolation relay solder breakdowns).

### Safety Alert Panel
- Displays high-priority electrical risks and mandatory class warnings (e.g. insulated safety glove requirements).
