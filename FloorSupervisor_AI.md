# Floor Supervisor AI Engine Specification

This document details the Gemma-powered copilots running inside the **Floor Supervisor Workspace**.

## 1. Copilots Design

### Resource Allocation AI
- Matches technicians to incoming job cards by cross-referencing vehicle models, certifications (e.g. EV Gold vs Mechanical Bronze), and current load parameters.

### Sequence Optimization Engine
- Outlines optimal stage execution flows (e.g. diagnostic isolation before mechanical works) to reduce overall waiting times.

### Delay & Rework Risk Predictors
- Checks parts availability and historic rework rates to flag high-risk jobs.
