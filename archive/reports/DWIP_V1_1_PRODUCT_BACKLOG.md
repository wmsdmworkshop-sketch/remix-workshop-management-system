# DWIP V1.1 – PRODUCT BACKLOG & EVOLUTION ROADMAP

---

## 1. Governance Note
This document specifies the planned product backlog for **Version 1.1**. No items listed here shall be implemented in the stable `v1.0.0` release baseline.

---

| Backlog Item | Category | Business Value | Priority | Complexity | Target Sprint |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **AI Predictive Part Replacement Engine** | AI Features | Recommends preventive part replacements based on fleet wear models | High | Medium | Sprint 1.1.1 |
| **Document AI OCR Auto-Intake** | Document AI / OCR | Auto-extracts registration & odometer from uploaded photos | High | Medium | Sprint 1.1.1 |
| **Voice Assistant for Technicians** | Voice Assistant | Hands-free job card task logging on floor | Medium | High | Sprint 1.1.2 |
| **IoT Telemetry Sensor Gateway** | IoT | Real-time vehicle OBD-II diagnostic streaming | Medium | High | Sprint 1.1.3 |
| **Fleet Predictive Maintenance Insights** | Fleet Intelligence | Predicts breakdown risks across 1,000+ fleet vehicles | High | High | Sprint 1.1.2 |
| **Automated Parts Re-order Intelligence** | Inventory Intelligence | Auto-generates POs when stock breaches min thresholds | High | Medium | Sprint 1.1.3 |
| **Native Mobile App (Android/iOS)** | Mobile App | Offline-capable mobile app for field technicians | High | High | Sprint 1.1.4 |
| **Real-time Push Notification Engine** | Notifications | Instant WhatsApp / SMS status alerts to vehicle owners | High | Low | Sprint 1.1.1 |
