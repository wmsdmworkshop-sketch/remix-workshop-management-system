# Technician Workspace Accessibility

This document outlines the accessibility compliance standards met by the **Technician Workspace**.

## 1. Compliance Standards

### Large Touch Targets
- Button controls (Start, Pause, Complete, checklist items) feature at least 48x48px touch sizes for hand-held tablets.

### Contrast & Contrast Ratio
- Focus outlines and status badges conform to high contrast specifications on dark themes.

### Semantic Elements
- Uses standard headings and forms containing distinct ID parameters for verification tests.
