Access requires credentials issued by the dealership, and the app has no self-service registration or public sign-up.

This policy explains what the app collects, why, where it goes, and how long it is kept.

2. What the app collects, and why

2.1 Employee account information

Your name, employee ID, assigned role, branch, and work contact details are held in the dealership's employee directory. They are used to authenticate you, to apply role-based access controls, and to attribute actions you take in the workshop record (for example, who opened or approved a job card).

2.2 Camera and photographs

The app requests camera access so you can photograph vehicles at gate entry and during service — number plates, chassis plates, odometer readings, damage, and job documentation. Images are uploaded to the dealership's servers and attached to the relevant vehicle or job card record. Number plate and chassis images are additionally sent to automated text recognition services (see section 4) so the fields can be filled in automatically.

The app does not access your device's photo library beyond images you explicitly select, and it does not scan, index, or upload your personal photos.

2.3 Precise location

The app requests precise (GPS) location and uses it only for these three purposes:

  • Gate entry verification — confirming a vehicle intake was recorded at the workshop premises.
  • Attendance and shift logging — confirming that a clock-in or clock-out occurred at an authorised site.
  • Roadside assistance dispatch — locating a vehicle or responder during a breakdown call.

Location is captured at the moment you perform one of these actions. The app does not track your location continuously, does not collect location while it is closed or in the background, and does not use location for advertising or profiling.

2.4 Vehicle and customer business records

In the course of your work the app processes dealership business data — vehicle registration and chassis numbers, owner and fleet contact details, service history, estimates, and invoices. This data belongs to the dealership and its customers, not to you, and is governed by the dealership's customer-facing terms.

2.5 Technical and diagnostic data

Server logs record request timestamps, IP address, app version, and error traces. These are used for security monitoring, fault diagnosis, and audit.

3. What the app does not do

  • No advertising, ad identifiers, or ad networks.
  • No sale or rental of personal data to any third party.
  • No behavioural profiling or cross-app tracking.
  • No background or continuous location collection.
  • No access to contacts, SMS, call logs, or microphone.

5. Legal basis

Processing of employee data is necessary for the performance of your employment and for the legitimate operation, safety, and audit of the workshop. Processing of vehicle and customer data is necessary to deliver the service the customer has requested.

6. Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Access is controlled by authenticated sessions and role-based permissions, so you see only what your role requires. Privileged actions — overrides, approvals, deletions — are written to an immutable audit log. Data at rest is stored on encrypted managed infrastructure.

7. Retention

Workshop, job card, invoice, and warranty records are retained for as long as required by the dealership's statutory, tax, and manufacturer-warranty obligations. Employee account and attendance records are retained for the duration of employment and for the period afterwards required by labour and tax law. Server diagnostic logs are retained for a short operational window and then rotated out.

8. Your rights

Subject to the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law, you may request access to the personal data we hold about you, correction of inaccurate data, erasure where no legal retention duty applies, and redressal of grievances.

To exercise any of these rights, or to request deletion of your account and associated personal data, write to privacy@aivaahan.com from your registered address. We will respond within the period prescribed by law. Note that requests to erase business records may be refused where we are legally obliged to retain them.

9. Children

This is a workplace application issued only to employees. It is not directed at, and must not be used by, anyone under 18.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top of this page and, where the change is significant, notify users within the app.
