# DWIP Training Manual
**Dealership Operational Staff Curriculum**

## 1. Module 1: Job Cards Lifecycle Management
* **Objective**: Familiarize Service Advisors and Workshop Supervisors with state updates.
* **Key Tasks**: Creating a customer profile, mapping vehicle VINs, setting priority, and submitting claims.

## 2. Module 2: Analytics & Business Programs
* **Objective**: Train managers on utilizing the KPI Metrics Catalog.
* **Key Tasks**: Resolving contract renewal rules for AMC policies, inspecting parts margins, and generating branch revenue trends.
