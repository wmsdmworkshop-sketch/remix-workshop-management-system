# DWIP User Manual
**Dealership Operations Team User Guide**

## 1. Service Advisor Guide
1. **Job Card Creation**: Log into the advisor portal, enter the customer's mobile number, and enter repair complaints.
2. **Estimates & Approvals**: Add estimated spare parts and labor rates, and click "Submit for Approval".

## 2. Floor Supervisor & Technicians
1. **Allocation**: Select an available Mechanical/Preventive bay.
2. **Technician Clock-in**: Click "Start Repair" to mark work status as `In Progress` in the event engine telemetry.
3. **Quality Check**: Complete diagnostic checklists to close the task.
