# Service Advisor AI Assistant Specification

This document details the Gemma-powered copilots running inside the **Service Advisor Workspace**.

## 1. Copilots Design

### Complaint Analysis & Symptom check
- Processes text inputs and matches symptoms against historical job codes to recommend matching tasks (e.g. EV battery calibrations).

### Parts & Labour Recommender
- Suggests synthetic oils, isolator shields, or mechanical gasket kits matching the registered complaints, reducing checkout times.

### Upsell Opportunities Engine
- Predicts package upsell chances (e.g. HEPA filter upgrades, wheel balancing) based on vehicle age and model parameters.
