# Executive Dashboard AI Engine

This document defines the AI models and algorithms running in the **Executive Operations Command Center**.

## 1. Copilots Design

### Enterprise Revenue Forecast
- Forecasts end of day revenue achievement ratios across all 5 branches by evaluating live invoices and factoring in queue volumes at a 70% pipeline transition rate.

### Resource Reallocation Advisor
- Analyzes technician loads and certification levels across branches to suggest cross-location technician deployments during repair volume surges.

### Daily Executive Brief
- Generates localized bottlenecks summaries, fleet customer risks warnings, and recommended priority actions for the GM Service.
