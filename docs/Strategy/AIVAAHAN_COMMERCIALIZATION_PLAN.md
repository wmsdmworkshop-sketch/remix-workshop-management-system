# AiVaahan Commercialization & GTM Strategy

**Platform**: AiVaahan Commercial SaaS Platform  
**Reference Customer**: Devanand Automobiles (Tenant 001)

---

## 1. Go-to-Market (GTM) Strategy

### Phase 1: Reference Customer Validation (Q3 2026)
- Stabilize Devanand Automobiles (`DLR-MUM-01`) as Reference Tenant 001.
- Publish Google Play Closed Testing Beta build (`v1.0.0-RC1`).
- Benchmark operational efficiency gains (25% TAT reduction, 100% QRT reach SLA compliance).

### Phase 2: Commercial Vehicle (CV) Dealership Expansion (Q4 2026)
- Target top 50 commercial vehicle dealership groups across India.
- Position AiVaahan as the pre-integrated workshop operating system supporting Tata Motors and multi-OEM fleets.

### Phase 3: Independent Workshop & Fleet Network (Q1-Q2 2027)
- Launch self-service onboarding portal for independent fleet repair centers.

---

## 2. Pricing & Monetization Model

1. **Per-Branch Subscription Fee**: Monthly recurring subscription per active workshop branch.
2. **Usage-Based Overages**: Tiered pricing based on monthly Job Card throughput.
3. **QRT Dispatch Add-on**: Per-vehicle breakdown dispatch tracking fee for non-Enterprise tiers.
