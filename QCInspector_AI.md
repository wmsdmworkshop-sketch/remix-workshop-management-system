# Quality Control AI Copilot Engine Specification

This document details the Gemma-powered copilots running inside the **Quality Control Inspector Workspace**.

## 1. Copilots Design

### Defect Risk Predictor
- Cross-references vehicle model parameters and wash schedules to check battery isolation safety risks.

### Checklist Completeness Auditor
- Cross-checks completed repair instructions to suggest missing inspections (e.g. rear caliper torques).

### Warranty Risk checker
- Validates warranty status, alerting inspectors to coverage updates.
